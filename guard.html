guard

Your positions work when you sleep.

Arm a condition, close the laptop. A network of guards signs when it fires — and nothing wider.

How a guard fires

001

You arm it

wake "ls positions | grep MNGO | close" --below 0.92
002

The watch begins

Guards poll for your condition. They see the intent and the trigger — never the key that made it.

003

It fires once

When the condition holds, a guard submits. Anything outside what you signed, the chain rejects.

condition met → guard signs → submitted

The four commands

nap

Defer a task. Once or forever on a repeat.

nap rent-check --every 30d

wake

Execute on a condition, with or without you.

wake "<pipe>" --below 0.92

watch

Monitor and notify. Nothing else. Free while the terminal is open.

watch MNGO/USDC --below 0.92
